# Brag Plan: Markoby

## What is this app?
Markoby is an AI growth marketer for the zero-ad-budget era that interviews founders about their product, builds platform-native organic distribution playbooks, and surfaces real high-intent prospects across Reddit, X, Discord, Instagram, and YouTube.

## The angle
"Your first 1,000 customers shouldn't cost a rupee of ad spend." Instead of burning money on PPC or Meta ads before finding product-market fit, Markoby replicates a senior growth marketer: sharp diagnostic questions, zero-fluff strategy, and automated distribution plans with posts tailored to survive platform culture (value-first on Reddit, build-in-public on X, non-salesy on Discord).

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A cinematic dark-mode reveal with electric lime neon glow:
Badge: `● Built for zero-ad-budget founders`
Headline slam:
**Your first 1,000 customers shouldn't cost a rupee of ad spend.**

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The 3-Step Growth Engine**: Drop your site URL (instant context crawl) → 5-min conversational AI interview → Platform-native playbooks.
- **The AI Marketer Diagnostic Dialogue**: An AI marketer asking the hard, unvarnished questions ("What's the #1 reason a founder would churn after week one?") and synthesizing zero-budget hooks in real-time.
- **Platform-Native Distribution Engine**: Tailored, rule-checked posts across 5 platforms with spam-rule guardrails (Reddit non-promotional value post, X viral 7-tweet breakdown, Discord community answers).
- **The Growth Outcome**: 3 weeks of automated day-by-day content calendar + high-intent scored prospects ready to reach today.

## Outro / punchline
The luminous electric lime **M** geometric mark lands with a crisp startup CTA:
"Start your first project free. No credit card required."

## User flow worth showing
1. Drop product URL → Auto-crawler extracts positioning and value props.
2. AI Marketer Dialogue → Live diagnostic interview tailored to founder logic.
3. Content Ops & Prospect Feed → Reddit & X rule-checked drafts + day-by-day calendar.
